What this doesStart here

Arrival Desk reads tomorrow's Specialty Arrival Report and turns it into the prep work: who to call, what to say, and a ready-to-send email for every reservation — plus one roundup email for Security.

It replaces opening the report, hunting for the tours that need early contact, and rebuilding each email from a Word template by hand.

Nothing leaves this device. Guest names, emails, phone numbers, and the report itself stay in this browser tab and disappear when you close or refresh it. Only the shared tour settings come from the server.

Getting started2 minutes
  1. Set tomorrow's park statusOperational or Non-Operational, at the top of the page. This decides which tours appear, which instructions the guest gets, and who lands on the Security email. Get it wrong and the wrong meeting location goes out — so set it first.
  2. Export the report as usualThe Specialty Arrival Report out of Crystal Reports / BusinessObjects, dated for tomorrow. Either export works — portrait or landscape.
  3. Drop the PDF on the boxDrag it in, or click to choose it. You can add both reports at once.
  4. Wait for the progress barThe portrait export reads instantly. The landscape export has no readable text inside it, so the tool reads it visually — that takes a bit longer and the bar will say so. Leave the tab open while it works.
Working one reservationRepeat per guest

Every tour in the report gets its own tab, and every reservation in that tab gets a card. Check every tab — the tab label shows how many reservations and how many tasks are left.

  1. Check the details firstGuest, contact, phone, email, party size, confirmation. Anything the tool isn't sure about is outlined in red and flagged at the top of the card. Open Compare against the report to see that exact row from the PDF, and type over anything wrong — your correction flows straight into the email.
  2. Call the guestUse the Call quick reference on the card: where they meet, what time to check in, what they'll ask about. Tap the phone number to dial.
  3. Open the email in OutlookOpen in Outlook copies the finished email — formatting, signature and all — and opens a new draft with the address and subject already filled in.
  4. Press Ctrl+V in the draftThat pastes the body and the SeaWorld signature. Then switch the From address to the Education address and double-check the recipient before you send.
  5. Attach the pass or directions if the card asksCards that need an attachment say so at the top. Download directions saves the PDF so you can attach it in Outlook on the web. Outlook draft + directions downloads a draft with it already attached instead.
  6. Send, then lock the tourThe last step has a Click to open Global Admin button that takes you straight to Global Reservations Admin in a new tab — lock it there, then tick the step. Ticking every step turns the card green and moves the counters at the top of the page. That's how you know nothing was missed.

Prefer your own draft? Copy email puts the formatted email on the clipboard without opening anything, and Show email displays the text so you can read it first.

The Security emailDo this last

The last tab builds one email to the dispatchers and security managers covering every program Security needs to know about tomorrow.

Each qualifying reservation card has an on security email checkbox. It's ticked by default — untick anything that shouldn't be on the roundup, then open the Security tab and send it the same way: Open in Outlook, Ctrl+V, check the From address, send.

Send it after you've worked the tours, so any corrections you made are already in it.

When something looks wrongFixes
A phone number or email looks scrambledExpected on the landscape export — it's read visually, so a stray character happens. Open Compare against the report, read the real value off the row, and type over the field. Better yet, use the portrait export, which is read exactly.
"No reservations match" — but there are tours tomorrowAlmost always the park status toggle. Flip it to the other mode. The message also lists which programs it did recognize in the file.
A tour is missing entirelyThe report may name it differently than the tool expects. A supervisor can fix the match text in Admin, then you re-upload the report.
Everything disappearedYou refreshed, or the tab closed. That's by design — nothing about guests is saved anywhere. Drop the report in again and rework the checklist.
Nothing happens when you drop the PDFCheck it's the Specialty Arrival Report and not another export, and that this machine is online — the reader libraries load from the internet even on a downloaded copy.
The email pasted as plain textThe signature needs a rich-text draft. Paste into the Outlook message body, not a subject or plain-text box, and use Ctrl+V rather than paste-as-text.
